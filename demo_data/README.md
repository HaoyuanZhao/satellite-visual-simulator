# Demonstration dataset

`demo_constellation/` contains deterministic, fictional data generated by
`python scripts/generate_demo.py`. It uses no external research files, user data,
credentials, or real satellite identifiers.

The sample has 24 frames, 96 satellites across two shells and eight orbital planes,
180 links and three example routes per frame, and three fictional ground stations.
Two links are marked degraded during frames 8–15, with illustrative route metrics.

Coordinates follow a simplified circular-orbit construction. Links, ground stations,
and performance metrics are illustrative; they do not represent an operational
constellation or validated orbital/communications simulation.
